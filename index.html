    Jailbreak Ludo

    Draw cards, not dice. Every token starts in jail.

    Mode
    Computer level
    Players
    Tokens each
    • +1 to +9 move a token forward (exact roll needed to finish)
    • -1, -2, -3 push back any token: yours or a rival's. They only join the draw once every player has a token past square 5
    • 🔓 Jailbreak frees a token. Stuck in jail? You get 3 draws
    • 🔄 Swap trade places with a rival
    • 💥 Eliminate send a rival to jail
    • 🛡️ Shield protects a token until your next turn
    • ⛔ Skip next player loses a turn
    • 🔁 Repeat play again
    • ⚡ Boost jumps you forward 5, 🕳️ Trap drops you back 4
    • 🔃 Reverse flips turn order, 🌋 Quake slides everyone back 3
    • Capturing or reaching home gives a bonus turn. Two of your tokens on one square can't be captured
    • ★ squares are safe from capture and card attacks
